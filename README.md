# 🌍 CHNOWA — What's Happening Around You?

CHNOWA is a modern local information platform designed to help people discover
what is happening around them in real time.

The goal is simple:

> 📍 Find the information that matters, where and when it matters.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📍 Local Information
Discover useful information around your selected location.

### 🚧 Road Alerts
See reported road works, blocked roads and traffic-related information.

### 🎓 Education
Discover university and education-related announcements.

### ⚡ Local Services
Find information about important local services such as water and electricity.

### 🎉 Events
Discover events and activities happening near you.

### 🗺️ Interactive Map
Visualize important information around your location.

### 📢 Community Reports
Users can report something happening around them and share it with the community.

### 🔔 Smart Notifications
Users will eventually be able to receive notifications about important events
and alerts in their selected areas.
